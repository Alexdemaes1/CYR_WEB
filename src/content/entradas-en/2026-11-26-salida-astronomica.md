## Introduction

The astronomy outing is an event organised by the Club de Ciencia y Redes, prepared during Science Week with the aim of bringing students from different degrees closer to a field as exciting as astronomy.

The activity will take place on **26 November 2026 in Aras de los Olmos**. The University will provide a bus leaving the Turia campus at 18:00. Once there, we will have access to the Universitat de València observatory, where we will be given a talk on various astronomical events and on what can be observed with the telescope. We will then have the chance to use the observatory telescope, always under supervision, to look at the night sky far beyond what the human eye can reach.

In addition to observing with the observatory telescope, the club will bring the University's telescopes to carry out its own observation. These are the same instruments we tested and calibrated in the recent solar observation session at the Turia campus, so they will arrive at the outing already checked.

To make the experience more immersive and accessible to everyone, we will also bring virtual reality headsets with the Stellarium app. It shows a map of a completely clear sky and includes information on clusters, constellations, planets and other phenomena, so the sky can be explored even if the weather does not cooperate.

Finally, as a thank-you for taking part, the club will give every attendee a shooting-star-themed keyring.

## Goals

The main goal is to promote science and show people from very different backgrounds what astronomy is about, through an outreach activity that is enjoyable and accessible, that everyone can enjoy and take a rewarding experience away from.

As complementary goals, the outing aims to:

1. **Bring research closer to students from different degrees**, regardless of their prior training.
2. **Put the club's preparatory work into practice**, in particular the use of the University's telescopes.
3. **Create a space for students to spend time together** around a shared interest.
