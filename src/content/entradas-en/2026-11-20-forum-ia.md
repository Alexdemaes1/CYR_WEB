## The forum

On Friday 20 November we are going to the 5th European Forum on Artificial Intelligence, at ADDA in Alicante. It is a morning of talks on artificial intelligence. The full programme is on [the forum's website](https://www.encuentrosnow.es/).

## Brasa, humo o chicha

This is the club's activity for the day. When you come out of each talk you sort it into one of three categories, *brasa* (a drag), *humo* (smoke, no substance) or *chicha* (real substance), and write a comment saying why. The page records the exact time you send it.

It is played on your phone, on [the activity page](/brasa/) (in Spanish).
